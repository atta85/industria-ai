# 3–5 Minute Hackathon Demo

## 0:00–0:30 — Introduce

“Industria-AI is not a chatbot with multiple names. It is a CrewAI-powered multidisciplinary analysis workflow. It dynamically selects the expertise relevant to a problem, researches evidence, challenges its own conclusions, and keeps a human in control.”

## 0:30–1:00 — Enter problem

Use:

> A production line is experiencing increasing dimensional defects after several hours of operation.

Run the scoping stage.

## 1:00–1:30 — HITL checkpoint 1

Show the AI interpretation. Explain that the human can confirm, correct or stop the workflow before specialists are activated.

Click **CONFIRM**.

## 1:30–2:30 — Multi-agent investigation

Point out the selected specialists. Explain that unnecessary agents are not activated. Show the research/evidence and critical-review stages.

## 2:30–3:15 — HITL checkpoint 2

Show the proposed analysis and deliberately enter:

> Consider whether temperature variation after prolonged operation could be contributing to dimensional drift.

Click **MODIFY / REANALYZE**.

Explain that human feedback becomes new analytical input.

## 3:15–4:00 — Verify

Review the revised proposal and click **ACCEPT**.

Show the Verified Decision Support Report and audit trail.

## 4:00–5:00 — Generality

Reset and briefly demonstrate a software problem. Point out that the router changes the specialist team rather than using a fixed manufacturing workflow.
